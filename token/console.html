    On-chain events

    Regulated by design. Identities are checked before every transfer, using a KYC hash only (no personal data on-chain). Transfers are limited to permitted jurisdictions and to a holder-of-record cap of 2,000. Employees face a lock-up to IPO plus 180 days. A 25% concentration limit applies, and the issuer can pause the token, with a written reason.

    Ethical by design. Grants come from a board-approved pool with a hard dilution ceiling. Revoking a grant cancels only the unvested part. The employee, not the company, decides when to claim. Forced transfers, freezes and recoveries cannot run without a written reason, and each one is logged. NAV is published with the hash of the valuation model behind it.
